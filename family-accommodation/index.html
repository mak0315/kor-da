Family Stay Checklist — What to Look For

Essential Family Amenities (All Verified on Kor Da)

  • 2+ bedrooms with proper beds (not sofa beds)
  • Full kitchen: stove, fridge, microwave, cookware, utensils
  • Parking space for family vehicle
  • High-speed WiFi for work/school/entertainment
  • Air conditioning in all rooms
  • Washing machine (essential for children's clothes)
  • Geyser/hot water for baths
  • UPS or generator backup (critical in Pakistan)
  • Safe neighborhood / gated community
  • Near park, playground, or open space for children
  • Proximity to hospital/clinic (especially with kids/elderly)
  • CNIC-verified host (NADRA Verisys) — mandatory on Kor Da

FAQ

Family Accommodation — Common Questions

What makes an accommodation family-friendly on Kor Da?

Family-friendly properties on Kor Da have: 2+ bedrooms, full kitchen (for home-cooked meals), parking (for family vehicle), WiFi & AC, washing machine (for laundry), safe neighborhood, proximity to parks/schools/hospitals, and space for children to play. Many also have UPS/generator backup, balcony, and study desk.

Which Islamabad areas are best for families?

Top family areas in Islamabad: G-11 (Metro access, parks, markets, parking), G-13 (affordable, government employee housing, quiet), DHA Islamabad (gated, secure, parks, amenities), Bahria Town (gated, community feel, parks, schools), F-11 (modern, Margalla views, good connectivity), F-10 (central, universities, markets).

Can I book family accommodation for a month or longer?

Yes. Monthly stays (28+ nights) receive 20-30% discounted rates. Popular for: government postings (1-3 months), families visiting relatives long-term, medical treatments requiring extended stay, corporate relocations. Contact us on WhatsApp for monthly rates and availability.

Are family accommodations near hospitals and schools?

Yes. G-8/G-9 near PIMS Hospital. G-10/G-11 near Shaukat Khanum & Aga Khan. E-11 near NUST/FAST. G-13/G-14 near multiple schools. DHA & Bahria Town have their own schools, hospitals, and parks within the community. Filter by 'Near Hospital' or 'Family' category on Explore.

What's the price range for family accommodation in Islamabad?

2-bedroom family apartment: PKR 6,500-8,500/night. 3-bedroom house/villa: PKR 10,000-25,000/night. Guest house (multi-room): PKR 5,000-12,000/night. Monthly rates: 20-30% discount. Rawalpindi (DHA, Bahria Town) offers 15-25% better value for similar space.

Can I book a family stay in Rawalpindi instead of Islamabad?

Absolutely. Rawalpindi (DHA, Bahria Town, Saddar) offers larger family homes at 15-25% lower prices. Easy access to Islamabad via Metro Bus (20 min) and Islamabad Expressway (15 min). Many families prefer Rawalpindi for month-long stays due to better value and community feel.
