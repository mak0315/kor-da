## Accommodation Types for NUST Visitors

### 1. E-11 Studios & 1-Beds — Best for Solo Parents/Students
- **Distance:** Walking to E-11 campus, 8-10 min drive to H-12
- **Format:** Compact studio or 1-bed with kitchenette
- **Price:** PKR 3,500–5,500/night
- **Guests:** 1-2
- **Best for:** Student visiting for exam/admission, solo parent dropping child
- **Amenities:** WiFi, AC, kitchenette, study desk, parking (some)

### 2. G-11 2-Bed Apartments — Best for Families
- **Distance:** 5-7 min drive to E-11, 10-12 min to H-12
- **Format:** Full 2-bed apartment with kitchen, living, parking
- **Price:** PKR 5,500–8,000/night
- **Guests:** 3-5
- **Best for:** Family staying during admissions week, convocation
- **Amenities:** Full kitchen, WiFi, AC, parking, washing machine, Metro access

### 3. G-10 Modern Apartments — Balanced Option
- **Distance:** 8-10 min drive to E-11, 12-15 min to H-12
- **Format:** Modern 1-2 bed apartments
- **Price:** PKR 5,000–8,500/night
- **Guests:** 2-4
- **Best for:** Visiting faculty, researchers, longer stays
- **Amenities:** Full kitchen, WiFi, AC, parking, building security, Metro

### 4. H-12/H-13 Premium — Closest to Main Campus
- **Distance:** Walking to H-12 main gate
- **Format:** Newer 1-2 bed apartments, some serviced
- **Price:** PKR 6,000–12,000/night
- **Guests:** 2-4
- **Best for:** Visiting faculty, conferences, premium preference
- **Amenities:** Gym, pool (some), high-speed WiFi, parking, modern finishes

## Booking by Visit Purpose

### Admissions Season (July–September, January–February)
- **Book 4-6 weeks early** — highest demand
- **E-11 & G-11 fill first** — closest & most affordable
- **Stay length:** 3-7 days typical
- **Budget tip:** E-11 studios best value for solo parents

### Exams (Mid-semester, Finals)
- **Book 2-3 weeks early**
- **Stay length:** 1-2 weeks
- **Need:** Quiet, reliable WiFi, study desk
- **G-10/G-11** best balance of quiet + access

### Convocation / Graduation
- **Book 4-8 weeks early** — families book early
- **Stay length:** 2-4 days
- **Need:** 2-3 bedrooms for extended family
- **G-11, H-12, F-11** for family-sized units

### Visiting Faculty / Researchers
- **Monthly stays** — negotiate 20-30% discount
- **Need:** Workspace, high-speed WiFi, printing access
- **H-12/H-13, G-10** best for work environment
- **Contact via WhatsApp** for monthly rates

### Parents Dropping Off Freshman
- **Stay length:** 1-3 days
- **Need:** Simple, close, budget-friendly
- **E-11 studios** ideal — walk to campus, PKR 3,500-4,500
