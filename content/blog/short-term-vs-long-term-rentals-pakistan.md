Deciding between a short-term rental and a long-term lease in Pakistan? This complete comparison covers costs, flexibility, amenities, verification, and which option suits your situation.

## Quick Comparison Table

| Factor | Short-Term Rental (Kor Da) | Long-Term Lease (Traditional) |
|--------|---------------------------|------------------------------|
| **Minimum Stay** | 1 night | 11 months (standard) |
| **Maximum Stay** | Flexible (monthly discounts) | 1-3 years (renewable) |
| **Furnishing** | Fully furnished | Usually unfurnished |
| **Kitchen** | Fully equipped | Empty — you provide everything |
| **Utilities** | Included in price | Separate bills (electricity, gas, water, internet) |
| **WiFi** | Included | You arrange & pay |
| **Maintenance** | Host/property manager | Tenant responsible (usually) |
| **Security Deposit** | PKR 5,000–20,000 (varies) | 2-3 months' rent advance |
| **Advance Rent** | First stay only | 3-6 months advance common |
| **Agent Fee** | None on Kor Da | 1 month rent (standard) |
| **Verification** | CNIC via NADRA Verisys | CNIC copy, often informal |
| **Payment** | EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Bank | Cash, bank transfer |
| **Pricing** | PKR/night, weekly/monthly discount | PKR/month fixed |
| **Exit Flexibility** | Leave anytime (per booking terms) | Notice period (1-3 months), penalty |
| **Best For** | Temporary, uncertain, flexible needs | Permanent residence, known duration |

## Cost Comparison Example: 3-Month Stay in Islamabad (2-Bed Apartment)

### Short-Term Rental (Kor Da)
- Nightly rate: PKR 7,500
- Monthly rate (30% discount): PKR 7,500 × 21 nights = **PKR 157,500/month**
- 3 months: **PKR 472,500**
- Utilities, WiFi, maintenance: **Included**
- Security deposit: PKR 10,000 (refundable)
- **Total 3-month cost: ~PKR 482,500**

### Long-Term Lease
- Monthly rent: PKR 60,000
- 3 months rent: PKR 180,000
- Advance (3 months): PKR 180,000
- Security deposit (2 months): PKR 120,000
- Agent fee (1 month): PKR 60,000
- Furniture & appliances: PKR 200,000+ (one-time)
- Utilities (3 months): PKR 30,000–50,000
- WiFi (3 months): PKR 6,000–9,000
- Maintenance reserve: PKR 15,000
- **Total 3-month cost: ~PKR 791,000+**

**Short-term rental saves ~PKR 300,000 for a 3-month stay** — and you keep flexibility.

## When to Choose Short-Term Rental

✅ **Ideal scenarios:**
- Business trips (1 week – 3 months)
- Medical treatment (1 week – 2 months)
- University admissions/exams (2-4 weeks)
- Government posting (1-3 months before permanent housing)
- Family visiting relatives (2 weeks – 2 months)
- Relocation scouting (try areas before committing)
- Tourism/leisure (3 days – 2 weeks)
- Remote work trial (1-3 months)
- Waiting for home construction/renovation
- Digital nomad / flexible lifestyle

## When to Choose Long-Term Lease

✅ **Ideal scenarios:**
- Permanent job in city (2+ years)
- Family settling long-term
- Known duration > 1 year
- Want to customize/renovate space
- Have own furniture & appliances
- Prefer fixed monthly budget
- Building equity/rental history
