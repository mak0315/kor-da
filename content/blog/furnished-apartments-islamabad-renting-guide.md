Renting a furnished apartment in Islamabad for a short stay? Here's everything you need to know before booking — what "furnished" actually includes, how pricing works, which areas suit your needs, and a verification checklist.

## What "Furnished" Means on Kor Da

Every furnished apartment on Kor Da includes these **standard amenities**:

### Bedroom
- Bed with mattress, pillows, clean linens
- Wardrobe or closet with hangers
- Bedside table(s) with lamp(s)

### Living Area
- Sofa/seating for stated guest count
- Coffee table
- LED TV (usually 32-50")
- WiFi router (high-speed included)

### Kitchen (Full)
- Stove (gas or electric)
- Refrigerator
- Microwave
- Electric kettle
- Cookware: pots, pans, baking tray
- Utensils: knives, spatulas, ladles, cutlery
- Crockery: plates, bowls, glasses, mugs
- Basic spices, oil, tea, coffee (varies by host)

### Bathroom
- Geyser/water heater
- Towels (bath, hand, face)
- Toilet paper starter roll
- Basic toiletries (soap, shampoo — varies)

### Utilities & Extras (Included in Price)
- Electricity, gas, water — **no separate bills**
- High-speed WiFi
- Air conditioning (all rooms)
- UPS or generator backup (critical in Pakistan)
- Building maintenance & security

## Common Premium Add-Ons (Varies by Property)

- Washing machine (in-unit or building)
- Dishwasher
- Iron/steamer + ironing board
- Study desk & chair
- Balcony/terrace furniture
- Parking space (often included)
- Daily/weekly housekeeping (extra fee)
- Gym/pool access (DHA, Bahria Town, Blue Area)

## Verification Checklist Before Booking

- [ ] Host is CNIC-verified (mandatory on Kor Da — check for verification badge)
- [ ] Photos match description (request video tour if unsure)
- [ ] All stated amenities listed (WiFi, AC, kitchen, parking, UPS)
- [ ] Verified guest reviews (only completed stays can review on Kor Da)
- [ ] Clear check-in/out times and process
- [ ] WhatsApp contact for host (direct communication)
- [ ] Cancellation/refund policy understood
- [ ] Monthly rate confirmed if staying 28+ nights

## Red Flags to Avoid

- Host asks for payment outside Kor Da/EasyPaisa/JazzCash/bank transfer
- No CNIC verification badge on profile
- Photos look like stock images (reverse image search)
- Price significantly below market rate
- Pressure to book immediately without questions
- No WhatsApp or direct contact provided
