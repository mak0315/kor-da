Traveling to Islamabad with family? Whether you're visiting relatives, on a medical trip, government posting, or vacation — finding the right family accommodation makes all the difference. This practical guide covers everything parents and family travelers need to know.

## Top Family-Friendly Areas in Islamabad

### 1. G-11 — Best Overall Value
- **Why:** Metro station, G-11 Markaz (markets, banks), parks, wide roads, parking
- **Property types:** 2-3 bed apartments, some houses
- **Price:** PKR 5,500–8,000/night
- **Nearby:** Metro Bus, Fatima Jinnah Park (10 min), schools, hospitals

### 2. DHA Islamabad — Premium Secure
- **Why:** Gated, 24/7 security, internal parks, playgrounds, community centers, schools
- **Property types:** Villas, townhouses, premium apartments
- **Price:** PKR 7,000–20,000/night
- **Nearby:** DHA schools, DHA hospital, DHA park, Monal (15 min)

### 3. Bahria Town — Community Feel
- **Why:** Master-planned, parks in every phase, Safari Park, cinema, malls, schools, hospitals inside
- **Property types:** Villas, apartments, townhouses
- **Price:** PKR 6,000–18,000/night
- **Nearby:** Bahria schools/hospitals, Safari Park, Centaurus Mall (20 min)

### 4. G-13 — Budget-Friendly Government Area
- **Why:** Quiet, government employee housing, affordable, Metro planned, near parks
- **Property types:** 2-3 bed apartments
- **Price:** PKR 4,500–6,500/night
- **Nearby:** G-13 Markaz, Fatima Jinnah Park, multiple schools

### 5. F-11 — Modern & Scenic
- **Why:** Newer construction, Margalla Hills views, good connectivity, modern amenities
- **Property types:** Modern 2-3 bed apartments
- **Price:** PKR 5,000–9,000/night
- **Nearby:** F-11 Markaz, Margalla Trail 5, Centaurus (15 min)

## Family Accommodation Checklist

### Must-Haves (Non-Negotiable)
- [ ] 2+ bedrooms with proper beds (not sofa beds for kids)
- [ ] Full kitchen: stove, fridge, microwave, cookware, utensils
- [ ] High-speed WiFi (for kids' entertainment, work, school)
- [ ] AC in **all** bedrooms + living room
- [ ] Washing machine (essential with kids)
- [ ] Geyser/hot water for baths
- [ ] UPS or generator backup
- [ ] Parking (if you have a car)
- [ ] CNIC-verified host (mandatory on Kor Da)

### Nice-to-Haves
- [ ] Balcony/terrace for kids to get fresh air
- [ ] Iron/steamer for uniforms/clothes
- [ ] Study desk for homework/remote work
- [ ] Building security guard
- [ ] Nearby park/playground (walking distance)
- [ ] Elevator (if upper floor with stroller)
- [ ] Bathtub (easier for toddlers)

## Pricing for Families (PKR/night)

- **2-bed apartment:** 6,500–8,500 (Islamabad), 5,500–7,500 (Rawalpindi DHA/Bahria)
- **3-bed house/villa:** 10,000–25,000
- **Guest house (multi-room):** 5,000–12,000
- **Monthly (28+ nights):** 20–30% discount

**Pro tip:** Rawalpindi (DHA, Bahria Town) offers 15-25% better value for similar space, with 15-20 min Metro/Expressway access to Islamabad.

## Booking Tips for Families

1. **Book early** — family units go fast during Eid, summer, school admissions
2. **Message host** about: crib availability, high chair, kid-friendly utensils, nearest pharmacy
3. **Confirm parking** — essential if driving from another city
4. **Ask about noise** — upper floors quieter, avoid main road-facing units
5. **Monthly stays** — negotiate directly via WhatsApp for 1-3 month government postings
