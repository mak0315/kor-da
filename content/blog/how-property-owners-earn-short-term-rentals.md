## Why Short-Term Rentals? (vs Long-Term Lease)

| Factor | Short-Term (Kor Da) | Long-Term Lease |
|--------|---------------------|-----------------|
| **Monthly Income Potential** | PKR 150k–500k+ (varies) | PKR 50k–180k fixed |
| **Occupancy Control** | You choose dates | Locked 11+ months |
| **Furnishing Cost** | One-time (you keep assets) | Tenant furnishes |
| **Maintenance** | Between guests (easier) | Tenant responsible |
| **Risk** | Diversified (many guests) | Single tenant risk |
| **Payments** | PKR, EasyPaisa, secured | Cash/bank, disputes common |

## Tax & Legal Considerations

### Income Tax (Pakistan)
- **Rental income** taxable under "Income from Property" (Section 15) or "Income from Business" if regular
- **Deductions allowed:** Property tax, insurance, repairs, maintenance, loan interest, depreciation
- **Filer status:** Lower withholding tax if active filer
- **Consult tax advisor** for your specific structure (individual vs company)

### Regulatory
- **Cantonment/Islamabad admin** — Check local bylaws for short-term rental registration
- **Society/RWA rules** — Some gated communities restrict short-term (DHA/Bahria generally allow)
- **Building management** — Confirm no restrictions in apartment buildings

### Contracts
- **Kor Da Terms** govern host-guest relationship
- **No separate lease** needed for short stays
- **Guest agreement** accepted at booking (digital)
