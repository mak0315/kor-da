## Accommodation Types Near PIMS

### 1. Guest Houses (G-8, G-9) — Most Popular for Medical Travel
- **Format:** Private rooms with attached bath, shared kitchen/living
- **Price:** PKR 3,500–5,500/night
- **Guests:** 1-3 per room
- **Best for:** Patient + 1 attendant, short stays (3-14 days)
- **Amenities:** WiFi, AC, geyser, shared kitchen, courtyard, parking (some)
- **Verified on Kor Da:** Yes — CNIC-verified hosts

### 2. 1-2 Bed Apartments (G-9, G-10, G-11) — Family-Friendly
- **Format:** Full apartment with kitchen, living, bedroom(s)
- **Price:** PKR 5,000–8,500/night
- **Guests:** 2-5
- **Best for:** Family staying with patient, longer stays (1-4 weeks)
- **Amenities:** Full kitchen, WiFi, AC, parking, washing machine, UPS

### 3. Serviced Apartments (G-10, Blue Area) — Hotel-Like
- **Format:** Apartment with housekeeping, reception, sometimes meals
- **Price:** PKR 8,000–18,000/night
- **Guests:** 2-4
- **Best for:** Those wanting hotel services with apartment space
- **Amenities:** Daily cleaning, room service, gym, concierge

## PIMS-Specific Booking Considerations

### For Patient Attendants (Most Common)
- **Priority:** Walking distance (G-8/G-9 guest houses)
- **Need:** Late check-in/out (surgery schedules), kitchen for medical diet, quiet for rest
- **Budget:** PKR 4,000–6,000/night typical
- **Stay length:** 3-21 days usually

### For Families Visiting Patient
- **Priority:** 2+ bedrooms, full kitchen, parking, WiFi for kids
- **Need:** Space for multiple family members, cooking facilities, laundry
- **Budget:** PKR 6,000–10,000/night
- **Stay length:** 1-4 weeks

### For Doctors/Medical Staff
- **Priority:** Quiet, reliable WiFi, workspace, parking
- **Need:** Short commute, flexible check-in, monthly rates
- **Budget:** PKR 7,000–12,000/night
- **Stay length:** 1-3 months (rotations)

## Booking Tips for Medical Travelers

1. **Book early** — G-8/G-9 guest houses fill fast during peak medical seasons
2. **Message host about:**
   - Late check-in (post-surgery, emergencies)
   - Attendant staying in patient's room
   - Kitchen access for special medical diet
   - Wheelchair accessibility (if needed)
   - Ambulance/ride access to PIMS gate
3. **Ask for photos of:** bathroom (geyser, grip bars), kitchen, bedding
4. **Monthly rates** — ask via WhatsApp for 2+ week stays (20-30% discount)
5. **Cancellation** — confirm policy for medical emergencies
