## Special Situations

### Monthly Stays (28+ nights)
- 20-30% discount standard
- Negotiate directly via WhatsApp
- Ask about: utility caps, cleaning frequency, maintenance contact

### Medical Emergency Stays
- Filter "Near Hospital" category
- Message host about: late check-in, attendant stay, kitchen for medical diet
- G-8/G-9 for PIMS, G-10/G-11 for Shaukat Khanum/Aga Khan

### Government Posting
- Mention "govt posting" for monthly rates
- G-11, G-13 popular with government families
- Ask about: parking for official vehicle, UPS backup

### University Admissions/Exams
- E-11 for NUST/FAST (walking distance)
- Book 2-3 weeks early for exam seasons
- Studios/1-bed most affordable for solo parents
