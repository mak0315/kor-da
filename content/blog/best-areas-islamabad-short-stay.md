Islamabad is a planned city with distinct sectors serving different needs. Here's our guide:

**F-7 Markaz** — Best for: Business travelers, diplomats. Premium dining, shopping at Jinnah Super, close to diplomatic enclave.

**F-11** — Best for: Modern living. Newer apartments with Margalla Hills views. Great WiFi, quieter than central sectors.

**G-8** — Best for: Medical travelers. Walking distance to PIMS Hospital. Affordable guest houses and apartments.

**E-11** — Best for: University visitors. Near NUST, FAST. Affordable studios and 1-bed apartments.

**DHA Islamabad** — Best for: Families. Gated community, secure, parks, great amenities.

**Blue Area** — Best for: Business. Corporate hub with luxury apartments and serviced flats.
