## Documents Required

### Short-Term (Kor Da)
- CNIC (guest) — verified at booking
- No lease agreement — booking confirmation only

### Long-Term Lease
- **Tenant:** CNIC (2 copies), passport photos (2), employer letter (if required)
- **Landlord:** CNIC, property ownership docs (registry/transfer), utility bills
- **Agreement:** Stamp paper (PKR 1,000+), notarized, registered (if >1 year)
- **Advance:** 3-6 months rent + 2-3 months security deposit
- **Agent fee:** 1 month rent (if using agent)

## Verification Checklist (Both Types)

### For Short-Term (Kor Da does this):
- [ ] Host CNIC verified via NADRA Verisys
- [ ] Property photos match reality
- [ ] Verified guest reviews available
- [ ] Amenities as listed
- [ ] Clear check-in/out process

### For Long-Term (You must do):
- [ ] Verify landlord CNIC matches ownership docs
- [ ] Check property registry/transfer letter
- [ ] Confirm no disputes/liens on property
- [ ] Test all utilities: electricity, gas, water, internet
- [ ] Document existing damage (photos + signed checklist)
- [ ] Verify building maintenance fund/status
- [ ] Check parking allocation (dedicated vs shared)
- [ ] Confirm utility meter ownership (separate vs shared)

## Negotiation Tips

### Short-Term (Kor Da)
- Ask for **monthly discount** (20-30% for 28+ nights)
- Negotiate **weekly rate** for 7-13 nights (10-15%)
- Request **late check-out** (often free if no next guest)
- Ask about **cleaning frequency** for monthly stays

### Long-Term
- **Rent:** Negotiate 5-10% below asking (standard)
- **Advance:** Push for 3 months instead of 6
- **Security:** Negotiate 2 months instead of 3
- **Agent fee:** Split 50/50 or landlord pays
- **Maintenance:** Landlord covers major (AC, plumbing, structure)
- **Paint/repair:** Get fresh paint before move-in
- **Renewal clause:** Fix 5-7% annual increase cap
- **Early exit:** 2-month notice, no penalty after 6 months

## Red Flags

### Short-Term
- Payment outside platform (cash, personal account)
- No CNIC verification badge
- Stock photos / reverse image search matches
- Pressure to book without questions
- Host unreachable after booking

### Long-Term
- Landlord refuses to show ownership docs
- Cash-only, no receipt
- No written agreement / stamp paper
- Excessive advance (6+ months)
- Verbal promises not in contract
- Building has utility disputes
- No parking confirmation in writing

## Islamabad-Specific Tips

1. **UPS/Generator** — Essential. Confirm backup hours & fuel policy
2. **Water supply** — Check if tanker-dependent (common in newer sectors)
3. **Gas pressure** — Winter issues in some areas; ask neighbors
4. **Internet** — Fiber available in most planned sectors; confirm provider
5. **Parking** — Dedicated slot vs street; get in writing
6. **Lift/Elevator** — Critical if floor 3+ with family/elderly
7. **Maintenance fund** — Ask society RWA about dues & upcoming projects
8. **Metro access** — G-11, G-10, I-8, I-10 have stations; premium for commuters
