FAQ

Islamabad Short Stays — Common Questions

Which areas of Islamabad does Kor Da cover?

Kor Da covers all Islamabad sectors including F-5 to F-11, G-6 to G-15, E-7, E-11, I-8, I-10, H-8 to H-13, DHA Phases 1-3, Bahria Town Phases 1-7, Blue Area, Diplomatic Enclave, Bani Gala, Gulberg Greens, Park Enclave, and Top City-1. Rawalpindi areas including Saddar, DHA, and Bahria Town Rawalpindi are also covered.

How much do short stays cost in Islamabad?

Short stays in Islamabad start from PKR 3,500/night for studios and go up to PKR 50,000/night for luxury villas. Average 2-bedroom apartment is PKR 6,500-8,500/night. Monthly stays receive discounted rates.

Are Kor Da hosts in Islamabad verified?

Yes. Every host on Kor Da is CNIC-verified via NADRA Verisys before their listing goes live. This applies to all Islamabad and Rawalpindi listings. No anonymous listings are allowed.

How can I pay for a short stay in Islamabad?

All payments are in PKR. Accepted methods: EasyPaisa (03495620844), JazzCash, bank transfer, and debit/credit cards. No USD or foreign currency payments.

Can I book monthly stays in Islamabad?

Yes! We offer daily, weekly, and monthly stays. Monthly stays get discounted rates. Contact us on WhatsApp 0315-5881733 for monthly pricing and availability.

What's the best area for medical travelers in Islamabad?

G-8 and G-9 are closest to PIMS Hospital. G-10 and G-11 are near Shaukat Khanum and Aga Khan. These areas have many guest houses and apartments catering to medical travelers and their families.

Where should university visitors stay in Islamabad?

E-11 is walking distance from NUST and FAST. G-11 and G-10 are near COMSATS and QAU. These sectors offer affordable studios and 1-bed apartments popular with visiting families during admissions and exams.
