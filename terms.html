6.1 Purpose

Kor Da connects guests with hosts for short-term accommodation. Occasionally something may go wrong with a booking — for example, a problem with the property, the service, access, payments, a cancellation, or the conduct of a party. This policy explains how such problems can be reported and resolved fairly.

6.2 Issues That Can Be Reported

  • Property not as described (missing amenities, inaccurate photos, wrong location)
  • Access issues (host unavailable, wrong keys, lockout)
  • Cleanliness or safety concerns (pests, mould, broken fixtures, security)
  • Host cancellation or failure to honour booking
  • Guest cancellation or early departure
  • Payment disputes (double charges, missing refunds)
  • Conduct issues (harassment, threats, unauthorised parties)

6.3 Issues Not Covered

  • Off-platform payments (no protection if you pay outside Kor Da)
  • Personal preference (e.g., "didn't like the view" if accurately described)
  • Force majeure (natural disasters, government orders) — handled case by case
  • Pre-existing conditions disclosed in the listing

6.4 Reporting Time

Report issues within 24 hours of check-in for property/access problems. For payment or cancellation disputes, report within 7 days.

6.5 How to Submit a Dispute

Message Kor Da via WhatsApp (0315-5881733) or email (kordapakistan@gmail.com) with booking reference, issue description, and evidence (photos, screenshots, messages).

6.6 Evidence and Documentation

Provide: booking confirmation, photos/videos of the issue, chat logs with host, receipts for alternative accommodation, any police/medical reports if applicable.

6.7 Host and Guest Response

Both parties have 48 hours to respond with their evidence. Kor Da reviews all submissions impartially.

6.8 Investigation Process

Kor Da reviews evidence, listing accuracy, chat history, and platform data. May request additional info. Decision within 5 business days.

6.9 Payment During a Dispute

Funds may be held during investigation. If resolved in guest's favour, refund issued to original payment method within 7 business days.

6.10 Possible Outcomes

  • Full refund (guest)
  • Partial refund (guest)
  • No refund (host)
  • Host payout upheld
  • Platform credit
  • Account warning/suspension for policy violations

6.11 Refund Decisions

Refunds follow the Refund & Cancellation Policy. Verified issues (not as described, access failure, safety) typically qualify for full refund. Guest-initiated cancellations follow the tiered cancellation policy.

6.12 Host Cancellation or No-Show

If host cancels or no-shows: full refund to guest + Kor Da assists with rebooking. Host may face suspension.

6.13 Guest Cancellation

Guest cancellations follow the tiered policy: Free cancellation 48h+ before check-in; 50% refund 24-48h before; no refund <24h before or no-show.

6.14 Off-Platform Transactions

Payments or bookings outside Kor Da void ALL protections. No dispute resolution, no refunds, no verification. Report off-platform requests immediately.

6.15 Fraud, Abuse, and Misrepresentation

Fake listings, stolen photos, identity fraud, or coordinated abuse result in permanent ban and potential legal action.

6.16 Safety and Emergencies

For immediate safety threats, call 15 (Police) or 1122 (Rescue) first, then notify Kor Da. We prioritise safety over process.

6.17 Appeals / Review

Parties may request one review within 7 days of decision with new evidence. Review decision is final.

6.18 Platform-Level Decisions

Kor Da may take platform-level action (delisting, suspension, commission adjustment) independent of individual disputes for systemic issues.

6.19 No Damage-Claim Program

Kor Da does not operate a separate host guarantee or damage insurance. Hosts should collect security deposits off-platform if desired.

6.20 Governing Law

These terms are governed by the laws of Pakistan. Disputes resolved in the courts of Islamabad Capital Territory.
