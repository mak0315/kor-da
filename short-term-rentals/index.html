FAQ

Short-Term Rentals Pakistan — Common Questions

What is a short-term rental in Pakistan?

A short-term rental in Pakistan is a furnished property (apartment, house, guest house, or villa) rented for short durations — typically by the night, week, or month. Unlike traditional long-term leases (11+ months), short-term rentals offer flexibility for travelers, business professionals, medical visitors, and families.

Which cities does Kor Da cover in Pakistan?

Currently, Kor Da actively covers Islamabad (all sectors) and Rawalpindi (Saddar, DHA, Bahria Town). Expansion to Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, and Quetta is planned. Northern areas (Murree, Hunza, Naran, Skardu, Swat, Chitral) are available under our Northern Areas category.

How are short-term rentals different from hotels?

Short-term rentals offer more space (full living room, kitchen, bedrooms), privacy (entire property to yourself), PKR pricing (no USD conversion), full kitchen facilities, and often better value for families, groups, and extended stays. Kor Da adds CNIC-verified hosts and secure PKR payments.

What payment methods are accepted for short-term rentals?

All payments on Kor Da are in Pakistani Rupees (PKR). Accepted methods: EasyPaisa (03495620844), JazzCash, bank transfer, and debit/credit cards. No foreign currency or USD payments required.

Can I book a short-term rental for a month or longer?

Yes. Kor Da offers daily, weekly, and monthly stays. Monthly stays receive discounted rates. Government postings, corporate relocations, and medical treatments often require 1-3 month stays which we accommodate.

Is Kor Da available in Karachi, Lahore, or other cities?

Karachi, Lahore, Peshawar, and Quetta expansion is planned. Currently we focus on Islamabad and Rawalpindi to ensure quality host verification and guest experience. Join our WhatsApp for updates on new city launches.
