Cookie Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help the site remember your preferences and improve your experience.

2. Cookies We Use

  • Essential: Required for core site functions, such as remembering your saved stays and form inputs.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity help us understand how visitors use the site (anonymous, aggregated data).
  • Local Storage: We store favorites and admin-panel session data in your browser's local storage, not in cookies.

3. Third-Party Cookies

We use Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity. These services set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

4. Managing Cookies

You can disable cookies in your browser settings. Note that disabling essential cookies may affect core features like saved favorites.
