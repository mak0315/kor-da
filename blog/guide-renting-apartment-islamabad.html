A Guide to Renting an Apartment in Islamabad

Kor Da Team · November 20, 2026

Renting an apartment in Islamabad? Whether you're looking for a short-term stay or a long-term lease, this guide covers everything — rental types, areas, budgets, verification, documents, and negotiation tips.

Rental Types in Islamabad

Short-term (Kor Da): Days to months, fully furnished, PKR, verified hosts, WhatsApp booking.
Long-term lease: 6-12+ months, usually unfurnished, advance rent + security, direct landlord.

Popular Areas by Budget (PKR/month for 2-Bed)

Premium (PKR 80k-150k+): F-7, F-6, Blue Area, E-7, Diplomatic Enclave.
Mid-range (PKR 50k-90k): F-10, F-11, G-10, G-11, G-8, I-8, I-10.
Budget (PKR 30k-55k): E-11, E-10, G-13, G-14, G-15, Bahria outer sectors.

Documents You Need

  • CNIC copy (tenant + guarantor)
  • Employment letter / business proof
  • References (previous landlord preferred)
  • Advance rent + security deposit (cheque/bank transfer)

Negotiation Tips

  • Ask for 1-month advance instead of 3-6
  • Negotiate maintenance clause (landlord pays major repairs)
  • Clarify utility billing (sub-metered vs shared)
  • Get rent escalation cap in writing (e.g., 5-10%/year)

Why Kor Da for Short-Term

Verified hosts, no advance rent beyond 1 night, WhatsApp booking, PKR payments, dispute resolution, flexible dates.
