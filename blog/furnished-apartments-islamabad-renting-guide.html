Furnished Apartments in Islamabad: What to Know Before Renting

Kor Da Team · November 16, 2026

Renting a furnished apartment in Islamabad for a short stay? Here's everything you need to know before booking — what "furnished" actually includes, how pricing works, which areas suit your needs, and a verification checklist.

What "Furnished" Means on Kor Da

On Kor Da, "furnished" means the apartment comes with: bed with linens, sofa, dining table/chairs, wardrobe, curtains, kitchen appliances (fridge, stove, microwave), basic cookware, AC, WiFi, geyser, TV. Always check the listing's amenity list for specifics.

Pricing by Area (PKR/night)

Premium (F-7, F-6, Blue Area): Studio PKR 5,000–8,000 | 2-Bed PKR 8,000–15,000
Mid-range (F-10, F-11, G-8, G-11): Studio PKR 4,000–6,000 | 2-Bed PKR 6,500–10,000
Budget (E-11, outskirts): Studio PKR 3,500–5,000 | 2-Bed PKR 5,500–8,000

Verification Checklist

  • Host CNIC-verified via NADRA Verisys (badge on profile)
  • Photos match actual property (video tour if available)
  • All amenities listed are present and working
  • Address is accurate on maps
  • Reviews from verified guests only

Red Flags

Cash-only demands, pressure to pay off-platform, blurry/stock photos, no address until payment, prices too good to be true.
