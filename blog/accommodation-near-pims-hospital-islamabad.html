Where to Stay in Islamabad When Visiting PIMS Hospital

Kor Da Team · November 21, 2026

Visiting PIMS (Pakistan Institute of Medical Sciences) Hospital in Islamabad for treatment, surgery, or to support a patient? This guide covers the best accommodation options nearby — from walking distance guest houses to family-friendly apartments — with verified listings on Kor Da.

Best Areas Near PIMS Hospital

G-8 (Walking Distance): 5-15 min walk to PIMS. Guest houses, studios, 1-bed apartments. PKR 4,000-8,000/night.
G-9 / G-10 (5-10 min drive): More apartment options, family-friendly. PKR 5,000-10,000/night.
F-8 / F-7 (10-15 min drive): Premium options, better dining. PKR 6,000-15,000/night.

What Medical Travelers Need

  • Proximity to hospital (walking or < 10 min drive)
  • 24/7 access for emergencies
  • Kitchen for special dietary needs
  • Quiet for patient recovery
  • Extra bed/sofa for attendants
  • Verified host for safety
