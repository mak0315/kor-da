What to Check Before Booking a Furnished Apartment

Kor Da Team · November 23, 2026

Booking a furnished apartment for a short stay in Pakistan? Don't risk your money or comfort. This essential checklist covers everything to verify before you pay — from host verification and amenity accuracy to payment safety and red flags.

Pre-Booking Verification Checklist

  1. Host CNIC Verified: Green "Verified" badge on Kor Da profile = NADRA Verisys passed.
  2. Multiple Real Photos: At least 8-10 photos showing every room, not stock images.
  3. Accurate Address: Drop pin on Google Maps — does it match listing area?
  4. Verified Reviews: Only guests with completed paid bookings can review on Kor Da.
  5. Complete Amenity List: WiFi, AC, kitchen, geyser, TV, parking, UPS — all confirmed.

Amenity Accuracy Check

  • WiFi speed (ask for screenshot of speed test)
  • AC in every room (not just living room)
  • Kitchen: fridge, stove, microwave, basic cookware
  • Geyser/water heater capacity
  • Backup power (UPS/generator) for outages
  • Parking if you have a car

Payment Safety

DO: Pay via Kor Da (EasyPaisa 03495620844, JazzCash, bank transfer, card). Get instant confirmation.
DON'T: Pay cash to host directly, use JazzCash/EasyPaisa personal numbers not linked to Kor Da, pay before verification, pay in USD.

Red Flags — Walk Away If:

  • Host pressures for immediate payment
  • No CNIC verification badge
  • Photos look like stock images or hotel rooms
  • Address is "shared after payment"
  • Price 50%+ below market rate
  • Host asks to communicate off-platform before booking

Contract & Cancellation

Read Kor Da's refund policy: Free cancel 48h+, 50% refund 24-48h, no refund <24h. Host cancellation = full refund + rebooking help.
