What to Check Before Booking a Short Stay in Pakistan

Kor Da Team · November 23, 2026

Booking a short stay in Pakistan — whether for business, medical, family, or tourism — requires due diligence. This comprehensive checklist covers verification, amenities, policies, payment safety, and red flags so you book with confidence.

✅ Quick Verification Checklist (Do These First)

  • Host identity verified (CNIC via NADRA Verisys on Kor Da)
  • Property ownership/authorization verified
  • Photos match reality (request video tour for 7+ night stays)
  • Verified guest reviews exist (completed stays only)
  • Price in PKR, all-inclusive (no hidden fees)
  • Payment via secure channel (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, Bank — not cash to stranger)
  • Cancellation/refund policy clear and in writing
  • Check-in process confirmed (key pickup, smart lock, host meet)

2. Photos & Listing Accuracy

Request from host (via WhatsApp):

  • [ ] Video walkthrough (slow pan, all rooms, bathrooms, kitchen, view, building entrance, street)
  • [ ] Recent photos (dated within 30 days)
  • [ ] Night photos (lighting, security, street)
  • [ ] Building exterior, lobby, elevator, parking, common areas

Verify: Reverse image search listing photos (Google Images/TinEye) to catch stolen photos.

5. Payment Safety

💰 Safe Payment Checklist

  • Pay ONLY via: EasyPaisa (03495620844), JazzCash, Bank Transfer to host's verified account
  • NEVER pay cash to unverified person, Western Union, MoneyGram, crypto
  • Get payment receipt/screenshot with: date, amount, host name, purpose, stay dates
  • On Kor Da: payment held until check-in confirmed (dispute window 24hr)
  • Confirm total price includes: all nights, cleaning, utilities, taxes — no surprises
  • Monthly stays: pay first month, then monthly — not full advance

6. Location Verification

  • [ ] Drop pin on Google Maps — confirm address matches listing
  • [ ] Check Street View (if available) for building exterior, street
  • [ ] Search area name + "reviews" / "safety" / "load shedding"
  • [ ] Distance to your destination (hospital, office, university) — test in Maps at likely travel times
  • [ ] Nearby: pharmacy, grocery, ATM, hospital, mosque, transport
  • [ ] Building security: guard, gate, CCTV, visitor log

  • Video call host if not meeting in person — confirm unit, keys, WiFi
