Short-Term Rentals in Islamabad: A Complete Guide

Kor Da Team · November 15, 2026

Islamabad, Pakistan's purpose-built capital, has seen a surge in short-term rental demand from business travelers, medical visitors, university families, and tourists. This complete guide covers everything you need to know before booking.

Why Choose Short-Term Rentals in Islamabad?

Short-term rentals offer more space, privacy, and value compared to hotels. You get a full apartment with kitchen, living room, and often a balcony — all at a lower nightly rate than comparable hotels.

Popular Areas for Short Stays

F-7 & F-6: Premium sectors near diplomatic enclave, Jinnah Super Market, upscale dining.
F-10 & F-11: Modern sectors with Centaurus Mall access, Metro Bus connectivity.
G-8: Medical district near PIMS Hospital. Affordable guest houses.
E-11: Near NUST/FAST universities. Budget-friendly studios.
Blue Area: Business district on Jinnah Avenue. Luxury serviced apartments.
DHA & Bahria Town: Gated communities with security and amenities.

Pricing Guide (PKR/night)

Studio: PKR 3,500–6,000 | 1-Bed: PKR 4,500–8,000 | 2-Bed: PKR 6,500–12,000 | Luxury: PKR 10,000–25,000+. Monthly stays get 20–30% discount.
