Short-Term vs Long-Term Rentals in Pakistan: Complete Comparison

Kor Da Team · November 19, 2026

Deciding between a short-term rental and a long-term lease in Pakistan? This complete comparison covers costs, flexibility, amenities, verification, and which option suits your situation.

Quick Comparison Table

FactorShort-Term (Kor Da)Long-Term Lease
DurationDays to months6-12+ months
FurnishingFully furnishedUsually unfurnished
Upfront Cost1 night + security3-6 months advance + security
UtilitiesIncludedTenant pays separately
FlexibilityHigh (cancel/modify)Low (penalties for early exit)
VerificationCNIC-verified hostsLandlord CNIC, registry check
CurrencyPKR onlyPKR (sometimes USD indexed)
MaintenanceHost/PlatformTenant/Landlord split

Choose Short-Term When:

  • Staying < 6 months (business trip, medical, study, relocation)
  • Need furnished, move-in ready place
  • Want flexibility to extend or leave early
  • Prefer all-inclusive pricing (utilities, WiFi, cleaning)
  • Value verified hosts and platform protection

Choose Long-Term When:

  • Staying 12+ months (permanent job, family relocation)
  • Want to furnish/decorate yourself
  • Lower effective monthly cost matters most
  • Comfortable managing utilities, maintenance
