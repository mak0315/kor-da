How to Find Temporary Accommodation in Islamabad

Kor Da Team · November 18, 2026

Need temporary accommodation in Islamabad? Whether it's for a business trip, medical treatment, university admissions, government posting, or family visit — this step-by-step guide helps you find the right place quickly and safely.

Step 1: Define Your Requirements

Dates, budget (PKR/night), number of guests, bedrooms needed, preferred area (near work/hospital/university), must-have amenities (WiFi, AC, kitchen, parking).

Step 2: Choose the Right Platform

Kor Da: CNIC-verified hosts, PKR pricing, WhatsApp booking, dispute resolution. Avoid: Unverified Facebook groups, OLX (no verification), Airbnb (USD pricing, limited local support).

Step 3: Filter by Area & Category

Business/Blue Area: F-7, F-6, Blue Area, F-10.
Medical/PIMS: G-8, G-9, G-10.
University/NUST: E-11, G-11, G-10.
Family/Government: DHA, Bahria Town, G-11, F-11.

Step 4: Verify Before Booking

  • Host has CNIC-verified badge on Kor Da
  • Multiple real photos (not stock images)
  • Accurate address on Google Maps
  • Verified guest reviews
  • Clear cancellation policy

Step 5: Book via WhatsApp

Message +92-315-5881733 with dates, guests, and property reference. Pay via EasyPaisa/JazzCash/bank transfer. Get confirmation instantly.
