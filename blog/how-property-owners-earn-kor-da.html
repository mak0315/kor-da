Kor Da Team · November 24, 2026

Turn your vacant or underutilized property in Islamabad/Rawalpindi into a steady income stream. Kor Da connects verified hosts with verified guests for short-term stays — no agent fees, PKR payments, local support. This complete guide covers everything from listing to maximizing earnings.

Step 1: Check Eligibility

Property types accepted:

  • ✅ Apartments (studio, 1-4 bed)
  • ✅ Houses (full or portion)
  • ✅ Guest houses / Farm houses
  • ✅ Serviced apartments
  • ✅ Farmhouses (Islamabad outskirts)

Requirements:

  • Property in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, or surrounding areas
  • Owner or authorized manager (CNIC verified)
  • Fully furnished with basic amenities
  • Legal ownership/authorization documents
  • Willing to accept PKR payments via EasyPaisa/JazzCash/Bank

Step 2: List Your Property

  1. Go to koradaa.com/host-your-property
  2. Fill the form: property type, address, bedrooms, bathrooms, max guests
  3. Upload documents: CNIC (front/back), ownership proof (registry/intiqal/utility bill)
  4. Add photos: 15+ high-quality photos (every room, building, amenities, view, street)
  5. Set amenities: WiFi, AC, kitchen, laundry, parking, elevator, backup power, etc.
  6. Submit → Kor Da team reviews within 24 hours

Step 7: Maximize Occupancy & Earnings

Photos That Convert

  • Daytime + nighttime shots
  • Every room: bedroom(s), bathroom(s), kitchen, living, balcony, view
  • Building: entrance, lobby, elevator, parking, security, amenities
  • Neighborhood: street, nearby landmarks, market, park
  • Details: AC remote, WiFi router, kitchen appliances, linens, toiletries
  • Minimum 15 photos, 1920px wide, well-lit, decluttered

Description That Sells

  • Lead with USP: "5-min walk to PIMS", "Metro station 200m", "Rooftop view of Margalla"
  • List all amenities explicitly
  • Mention: backup power hours, water tank capacity, internet speed
  • Target audience: "Ideal for medical attendants", "Perfect for NUST parents"
  • House rules clear: smoking, parties, visitors, check-in/out

Dynamic Pricing Tips

  • Weekend rates +15-25%
  • Holiday seasons (Eid, wedding season Nov-Feb) +50-100%
  • Exam seasons (NUST, medical) +20-30% near universities/hospitals
  • Last-minute (48hr) discount 10-15% to fill gaps
  • Monthly discount 20-35% for 28+ nights
  • Quarterly (90+) discount 25-35% negotiable

Get 5-Star Reviews

  • Welcome message with WiFi, check-in guide, local tips
  • Quality linens, towels, toiletries, basic kitchen supplies
  • Respond to messages within 1 hour (WhatsApp)
  • Proactive: "AC working well?" "Need anything?" mid-stay
  • Easy check-out: clear instructions, flexible time if no next guest
  • Ask for review: "Hope you enjoyed! A review helps us host better."

Common Host Questions

QuestionAnswer
Can I list if I have a long-term tenant?Yes, block those dates on calendar. Fill vacancy gaps.
What if guest damages property?Security deposit (PKR 5k-20k set by you) + Kor Da mediation. Document with photos.
Can I reject a booking?Yes, but avoid discrimination. Valid reasons: capacity, dates, verified status.
Who cleans between guests?Host responsibility. Include cleaning fee in nightly rate or separate.
What about utilities?Included in your nightly rate. Set rate to cover average usage.
Can I host myself (meet guests)?Yes, or use smart lock/key lockbox. Your choice.
Is there a contract?Host Terms of Service on signup. No exclusive lock-in.
How to pause listing?Dashboard → Calendar → Block all dates or toggle "Pause Listing".
