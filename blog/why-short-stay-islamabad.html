Why Short Stays in Islamabad Are Better Than Hotels

Kor Da Team · January 15, 2025

Islamabad has seen a rapid shift from traditional hotels to short-stay apartments and guest houses. Here's why:

More Space

A typical hotel room offers 250-400 sq ft. A short-stay apartment gives you a full living room, bedroom, kitchen, and often a balcony.

Full Kitchen

Cook your own meals. Save money on eating out. Especially important for families with children or medical travelers.

Privacy

Entire apartment to yourself. No shared corridors, no noisy neighbors, no housekeeping interruptions.

PKR Pricing

Pay in Pakistani Rupees. No USD conversion, no hidden foreign transaction fees.

Verified Hosts

Every Kor Da host is CNIC-verified via NADRA Verisys. You know exactly who you're booking from.
