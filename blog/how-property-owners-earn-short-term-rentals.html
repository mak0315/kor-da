How Property Owners Can Earn Through Short-Term Rentals in Pakistan

Kor Da Team · November 24, 2026

Own a property in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, or expanding cities? Turn it into a verified short-term rental income stream. This complete guide covers everything property owners need to know — from setup and pricing to verification, payments, tax, and maximizing occupancy on Kor Da.

Why Short-Term Rentals? (vs Long-Term Lease)

Higher yield: 2-3x monthly long-term rent. Flexibility: Use property yourself anytime. No tenant risk: No eviction hassles, non-payment. Platform handles: Marketing, booking, verification, payments, support.

Getting Started on Kor Da

  1. Apply via WhatsApp (+92-315-5881733) or /host-your-property/
  2. Submit CNIC, ownership proof, utility bills
  3. NADRA Verisys verification (24-48 hours)
  4. Professional photos arranged by Kor Da
  5. Listing goes live — start accepting bookings

Pricing Strategy

Daily rate: 2.5-3x monthly long-term / 30. E.g., if long-term is PKR 60k/month → daily PKR 5,000-6,000.
Weekly discount: 10-15% off daily.
Monthly discount: 20-30% off daily.
Seasonal: Increase 20-50% during peak (weddings, holidays, conferences).

Payments & Tax

All payments in PKR via EasyPaisa/JazzCash/bank transfer. Kor Da deducts commission on completed bookings only. You receive net payout. Consult tax advisor for rental income declaration.

Maximizing Occupancy

  • Professional photos — Kor Da provides
  • Complete amenity list (WiFi, AC, kitchen, parking, UPS)
  • Respond to inquiries within 1 hour
  • Maintain 4.8+ rating with quick issue resolution
  • Offer airport pickup, late check-in flexibility
