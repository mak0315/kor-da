Accommodation Near NUST Islamabad: Complete Guide for Visitors

Kor Da Team · November 22, 2026

Visiting NUST (National University of Sciences & Technology) Islamabad for admissions, exams, convocation, or as visiting faculty? This guide covers the best accommodation options near NUST's H-12 and Sector E-11 campuses — from walking-distance studios to family apartments.

Best Areas Near NUST

E-11 (Walking Distance to E-11 Campus): 5-15 min walk. Budget studios, 1-bed apartments. PKR 3,500-6,000/night. Near FAST University too.
G-11 / G-10 (5-10 min drive): More variety, family apartments. PKR 5,000-10,000/night. Metro Bus access.
H-12 / H-11 (Near H-12 Campus): Newer area, modern apartments. PKR 5,500-12,000/night.

What University Visitors Need

  • Proximity to campus (walking or < 10 min drive)
  • High-speed WiFi for online classes/work
  • Quiet study space
  • Kitchen for meals (saves money)
  • Flexible check-in for exam schedules
  • Verified host for safety

Transport Options

Metro Bus: G-11 station → ride to nearest stop, then taxi/rideshare to campus.
Ride-hailing: Careem, inDrive, Uber widely available.
University shuttle: NUST runs shuttles from some sectors.
