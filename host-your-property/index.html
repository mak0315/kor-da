What You Need to Get Started

Simple documentation — standard for any legitimate rental in Pakistan.

Valid CNIC

Verified via NADRA Verisys. Must match property ownership/authorization name.

Ownership / Authorization

Property registry, transfer letter, or written authorization from owner (if you're an agent/manager).

Utility Bill

Recent electricity or gas bill (last 3 months) for address verification.

Property Photos

5+ photos: bedrooms, living area, kitchen, bathroom, building exterior, view/balcony.

Amenity List

WiFi, AC, kitchen appliances, parking, UPS/generator, washing machine, geyser, security features.

Pricing Strategy

Nightly rate, weekly/monthly discounts, cleaning fee (if any), security deposit preference.

FAQ

Hosting on Kor Da — Common Questions

How much does it cost to list my property on Kor Da?

Zero. No listing fee, no monthly subscription, no hidden charges. We only take a small commission (9%) on completed bookings. You keep 91% of the booking amount. This is the lowest commission in Pakistan's short-term rental market.

What documents do I need to become a host?

Required: Valid CNIC (verified via NADRA Verisys), proof of property ownership or authorization to rent, recent utility bill (electricity/gas) for address verification. Optional but helpful: property photos, floor plan, amenity list.

How does CNIC verification work?

We verify your CNIC against NADRA's official Verisys database before your listing goes live. Only a registered Pakistani company can access Verisys. This ensures every host on Kor Da is a real, verified person — no anonymous listings.

How do I get paid as a host?

Payments are processed in PKR via EasyPaisa (03495620844), JazzCash, or bank transfer. After guest check-in, funds are held securely. On checkout, your share (91%) is transferred to your preferred PKR account. No USD, no foreign exchange, no international transfer delays.

What types of properties can I list?

Apartments (studio, 1-bed, 2-bed, 3-bed+), houses/bungalows, guest houses, villas, farmhouses, serviced apartments, portions. Property must be in Islamabad, Rawalpindi, or expanding areas. Must be furnished and ready for short stays.

How long does onboarding take?

We contact you via WhatsApp within 24 hours of form submission. CNIC verification typically completes same day. Your listing goes live within 24-48 hours of successful verification. Skip the form and WhatsApp us directly at 0315-5881733 for faster onboarding.

Can I manage multiple properties?

Yes. Our host dashboard supports multiple properties. Each property goes through individual CNIC verification. You manage all listings, bookings, and earnings from one dashboard.

What if a guest damages my property?

Our Terms & Conditions include host protection. Security deposits can be collected. Disputes are resolved within 24 hours via our Refund & Dispute Policy. Host insurance partnerships are in development.
