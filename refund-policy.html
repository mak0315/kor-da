Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Cancellation Before Check-in

Guests may cancel a booking at any time before the check-in time. Cancellations made before check-in receive a full refund of the booking amount, held securely via Safepay escrow.

2. Cancellation After Check-in

Cancellations made after check-in are not refundable for the nights already stayed. Unused nights may be refunded at the host's discretion, minus a PKR 500 processing fee.

3. Property Mismatch

If the property does not match the listing (different unit, missing advertised amenities, or serious defects), you may file a dispute within 24 hours of check-in. If confirmed, you receive a full refund for nights not stayed.

4. Host Cancellations

If a host cancels a confirmed booking, the guest receives a full refund and a PKR 1,000 credit toward a future booking.

5. Refund Timeline

Approved refunds are processed within 3–5 business days back to the original payment method (EasyPaisa, JazzCash, card, or bank transfer).

6. How to Request a Refund

Contact us on WhatsApp 0315-5881733 or email kordapakistan@gmail.com with your booking ID and reason. We respond within 24 hours.

7. Escrow Protection

All payments are held by Safepay (SBP-licensed) in escrow until 24 hours after confirmed check-in. This protects both guests and hosts.
