FAQ

Furnished Apartments — Common Questions

What does 'furnished apartment' include on Kor Da?

All Kor Da furnished apartments include: bedroom furniture (bed, wardrobe), living room furniture (sofa, coffee table), full kitchen (stove, fridge, microwave, cookware, utensils), WiFi, AC, TV, geyser/water heater. Many also include: washing machine, UPS/generator backup, parking, balcony, study desk, iron/steamer. Exact amenities listed per property.

Are utilities included in the rent for furnished apartments?

Yes. The nightly/weekly/monthly price on Kor Da includes all utilities — electricity, gas, water, WiFi, and building maintenance. No hidden utility bills. You pay the listed PKR price via EasyPaisa, JazzCash, or bank transfer.

Can I book a furnished apartment for just a few days?

Yes. Minimum stay is 1 night. You can book daily, weekly, or monthly. Weekly stays (7+ nights) typically get 10-15% discount. Monthly stays (28+ nights) get 20-30% discount. Contact host via WhatsApp for exact monthly rates.

What's the difference between a serviced apartment and a furnished apartment?

A furnished apartment on Kor Da gives you the entire property with all furniture and appliances. A serviced apartment typically adds hotel-like services: daily housekeeping, reception, concierge, room service. Some Kor Da properties offer housekeeping as an add-on — check individual listings.

How do I know the apartment matches the photos?

Every Kor Da host is CNIC-verified via NADRA Verisys. If the property materially differs from the listing, you can dispute within 24 hours of check-in via WhatsApp. We respond same day and review under our Refund Policy. Verified guest reviews also help confirm accuracy.

Are furnished apartments available in Rawalpindi?

Yes. Rawalpindi (Saddar, DHA, Bahria Town) has furnished apartments at more affordable rates than Islamabad. Easy access to Islamabad via Metro Bus and Expressway. Browse Rawalpindi listings on our Explore page.
