FAQ

Rawalpindi Short Stays — Common Questions

Which areas of Rawalpindi does Kor Da cover?

Kor Da covers key Rawalpindi areas including Saddar (central commercial area), DHA Rawalpindi (all phases), and Bahria Town Rawalpindi (all phases). We are actively onboarding more verified hosts across Rawalpindi.

How much do short stays cost in Rawalpindi?

Short stays in Rawalpindi start from PKR 3,000/night for studios in Saddar and go up to PKR 25,000/night for luxury villas in DHA and Bahria Town. Average 2-bedroom apartment is PKR 5,000-7,000/night. Monthly stays receive discounted rates.

Are Kor Da hosts in Rawalpindi verified?

Yes. Every host on Kor Da — including Rawalpindi — is CNIC-verified via NADRA Verisys before their listing goes live. No anonymous listings are allowed anywhere on the platform.

How can I pay for a short stay in Rawalpindi?

All payments are in PKR. Accepted methods: EasyPaisa (03495620844), JazzCash, bank transfer, and debit/credit cards. No USD or foreign currency payments.

Is Rawalpindi good for families visiting Islamabad?

Yes. Rawalpindi (especially Saddar, DHA, and Bahria Town) offers more affordable family accommodation with easy access to Islamabad via the Metro Bus and Islamabad Expressway. Many families choose Rawalpindi for longer stays due to better value.

How do I get from Rawalpindi to Islamabad?

Metro Bus (Peshawar Morr to PIMS/Secretariat), Islamabad Expressway (15-20 min drive), Murree Road, and IJP Road all connect Rawalpindi to Islamabad. Ride-hailing services (Careem, inDrive) operate across both cities.
